The approach

A DIGITAL HOME FOR A LOCAL STYLE STORY.

A considered online home for DE LA BOSS OUTFITS, a fashion retailer in Kehancha, Migori County. This proposal preview shows how the store could present its collection online — with a clear, personal route from discovery to a WhatsApp conversation.

Why this exists

A CLEARER WAY TO DISCOVER THE STORE.

Local fashion retail often lives in conversations, WhatsApp statuses and word of mouth. This build brings that energy into a focused digital home: a curated catalogue, straightforward filters, clear product pages and an ordering journey that stays personal.

It is a working preview, not a live commercial platform. Product names, prices, stock and orders are illustrative content prepared to show the experience. The final catalogue, photography and business details would be supplied and approved by the owner before launch.

The approach

THREE IDEAS BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE.

01

Make browsing feel calm

An editorial layout gives each piece room to breathe. Search, filters and sorting are there when needed, without turning the collection into a wall of noise.

02

Be clear at every step

Prices, stock and imagery are labelled as preview content. The storefront never implies that an order has been received, paid for or fulfilled by the business.

03

Keep the human handoff

The WhatsApp enquiry prepares a clear message, then leaves the conversation with the customer. No fake automation stands in for a real shop conversation.

Preview notes

WHAT THIS PREVIEW IS — AND ISN'T.

A clear scope keeps a proposal honest. These notes explain what is real, what is illustrative, and what would change before launch.

Independent proposal

This site is not the official website of DE LA BOSS OUTFITS. It has not been commissioned, approved or launched by the business. It was prepared by Thomas Hondo Chacha as a proposal preview.

Illustrative catalogue

All 25 products, prices, sizes, colours, stock levels and descriptions are preview data. They do not represent confirmed shop inventory, real pricing or available sizes.

Order requests, not orders

Checkout saves an order request in this browser only. No payment, M-Pesa request, email, SMS or WhatsApp message is sent automatically. No delivery or collection is arranged.

Local-only data

Bag contents, order requests, enquiries, newsletter entries and dashboard changes use this browser's LocalStorage. Clearing site data removes them. Nothing is stored on a server.

Preview dashboard

The store dashboard is deliberately unauthenticated and clearly marked as a prototype. It demonstrates catalogue, stock, order and customer workflows, but is not a secure production back office.

Photography & imagery

Editorial and product photographs are licensed stock imagery used to demonstrate art direction. They are not photographs of the shop, its team or its actual stock. Image credits appear in the project README.

The Google Business Profile and Facebook video supplied for context informed this proposal only. Their content, ratings, hours, posts and contact details have not been reproduced or treated as verified facts.
