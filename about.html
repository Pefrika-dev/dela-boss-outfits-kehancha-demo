The concept

A DIGITAL HOME FOR A LOCAL STYLE STORY.

This is an independent website concept prepared for DE LA BOSS OUTFITS, a fashion retailer in Kehancha. It explores how a considered online storefront could feel — without pretending the shop has already launched one.

Why this exists

A CLEARER WAY TO DISCOVER THE STORE.

Local fashion retail often lives in conversations, WhatsApp statuses and word of mouth. This concept shows what a calm, focused digital home could add: a curated sample collection, straightforward filters, clear product pages and a low-friction path to an enquiry.

The experience is built as a practical demonstration, not a finished commercial platform. Every product, price, stock figure and order shown is sample content created for the proposal. The store's real catalogue would replace it.

The approach

THREE IDEAS BEHIND THE EXPERIENCE.

01

Make browsing feel calm

An editorial layout gives each piece room to breathe. Search, filters and sorting are there when needed, without turning the collection into a wall of noise.

02

Be honest at every step

Sample prices, stock and imagery are labelled throughout. The demonstration never implies that an order has been received, paid for or fulfilled by the business.

03

Keep the human handoff

The WhatsApp enquiry prepares a clear message, then leaves the conversation with the customer. No fake automation stands in for a real shop conversation.

Read before you judge it

WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATION IS — AND ISN'T.

Clarity matters more than polish. These notes explain the boundaries of the concept so nobody mistakes a proposal for a live shop.

Independent concept

This site is not the official website of DE LA BOSS OUTFITS. It has not been commissioned, approved or launched by the business. It was created by Thomas Hondo Chacha as a proposal demonstration.

Sample catalogue

All 25 products, prices, sizes, colours, stock levels and product descriptions are sample data. They do not represent confirmed shop inventory, real pricing or available sizes.

No real orders or payments

Checkout saves a demonstration order in this browser only. No payment, M-Pesa request, email, SMS or WhatsApp message is sent automatically. No delivery or collection is arranged.

Local-only data

Bag contents, demonstration orders, enquiries, newsletter entries and admin changes use this browser's LocalStorage. Clearing site data removes them. Nothing is stored on a server.

Open admin demo

The admin dashboard is deliberately unauthenticated and clearly marked as a prototype. It demonstrates catalogue, stock, order and customer workflows, but is not a secure production back office.

Photography & imagery

Editorial and product photographs are licensed stock imagery used to demonstrate art direction. They are not photographs of the shop, its team or its actual stock. Image credits appear in the project README.

The Google Business Profile and Facebook video supplied for context informed the proposal only. Their content, ratings, hours, posts and contact details have not been reproduced or treated as verified facts.

The build

DESIGNED AND DEVELOPED AS A PROPOSAL.

This concept was designed and built by Thomas Hondo Chacha — Software Engineer. It uses HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript with LocalStorage, with no framework, build step, paid API or third-party dependency.
