Sizes & fit

MEASURE ONCE. ORDER WELL.

Standard body measurements for clothing and footwear, plus a simple way to take your own. Use it as guidance, then confirm the final size with the store.

Guidance, not a guarantee. These are standard body measurements, not measurements taken from specific garments in this preview. Fit varies by cut, fabric and brand. If you are between sizes, or the piece is fitted, confirm with the store before ordering.

HOW TO MEASURE.

Use a soft tape measure, keep it level and snug but not tight, and measure over light clothing. Stand relaxed and breathe normally.

01

Bust or chest

Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape level under the arms and across the shoulder blades.

02

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the navel. Keep one finger under the tape.

03

Hips

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of the hips and seat, roughly 20 cm below the waist.

04

Foot length

Stand on paper, mark the heel and the longest toe, then measure the distance in centimetres. Measure both feet and use the longer one.

BEFORE YOU CONFIRM.

Between two sizes?

Choose the larger size for a relaxed fit, or the smaller size if you prefer a closer cut. For tailored pieces, taking the waist and chest measurements to the store gives the most reliable answer.

Fitted, regular and oversized

Two garments with the same size label can fit differently. Where a piece is described as fitted or oversized in this preview, treat the label as a starting point rather than a final answer.

Still unsure?

Send your measurements and the piece you are considering to the store on WhatsApp or by phone. A short conversation is quicker than a return.

Need a second opinion? Share your measurements with the store and ask them to confirm the right size for the piece you want.
