Policies

YOUR DATA, PLAINLY.

No account, no server database, no tracking cookies. This page explains exactly what this preview saves in your browser — and how to remove it.

On this page

  • The short version
  • What is stored
  • What is not collected
  • WhatsApp links
  • Hosting
  • Your control
  • When the store goes live
  • Questions

The short version. This preview has no backend. Your bag, order requests, enquiries and newsletter sign-ups are saved in this browser only, under the delaboss.demo.v1. prefix. They are never uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload them to.

No accounts are created, no passwords are requested, and no payment details are collected.

WHAT IS STORED.

All of it lives in your browser's LocalStorage for this site. LocalStorage is not a cookie and is never transmitted with web requests.

LocalStorage used by this preview
Key What it holds
…cart The pieces in your bag, with size, colour and quantity.
…orders Order requests you create, including the name, phone number and delivery details you type.
…enquiries Notes you save through the contact form.
…subscribers Email addresses you submit to the newsletter form.
…products The preview catalogue, plus any edits made in the store dashboard.
…settings Store name, phone numbers, WhatsApp number, announcement text and dashboard preferences.
…activity A short dashboard log of recent preview actions.

Anyone with access to this browser profile or device can read that data. Do not enter anything sensitive into the preview forms — including real payment details, which the forms never ask for.

WHAT IS NOT COLLECTED.

  • No account, login or password — the dashboard is a preview workspace and is not a secure back office.
  • No payment information. No card numbers, PINs or M-Pesa credentials are requested anywhere on this site.
  • No analytics, advertising pixels or third-party tracking scripts are installed.
  • No cookies are set by this site for tracking or advertising.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to the business, because the preview has no connection to it.

The fonts and stylesheets are served from this same site — there are no third-party font or CDN requests.

WHATSAPP LINKS.

Buttons and links marked as WhatsApp open wa.me in a new tab with a message prepared for you. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp.

Once you are in WhatsApp, your use of that service is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one. The same applies to your phone's dialler for the tel: links.

HOSTING.

This preview is served as static files by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify processes standard technical request data — such as IP address, browser type and the page requested — to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

No application database sits behind this site, so there is no store of customer records on the server.

YOUR CONTROL.

Remove everything

Opening the store dashboard and choosing Reset preview data clears every key listed above and restores the original sample catalogue. You can also clear site data from your browser settings — look for "cookies and site data" and remove this domain.

Remove one thing

Clearing your bag, deleting an order request in the dashboard, or unsubscribing from the newsletter form removes that entry only.

Private or shared devices

Use a private window, and close it when you are finished. On a shared device, clear site data afterwards.

WHEN THE STORE GOES LIVE.

This page describes the preview only. A live store that takes real orders would need a proper privacy notice covering the business's identity, what customer data is collected, how long it is kept, who it is shared with, and how customers can request access or deletion.

In Kenya that work sits under the Data Protection Act, 2019, administered by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. This preview is not a substitute for that notice.
