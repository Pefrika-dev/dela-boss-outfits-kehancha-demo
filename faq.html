Ordering

Can I buy from this website right now?

No. This is a proposal preview for DE LA BOSS OUTFITS, not the official live store. The catalogue, prices, stock levels and order references are illustrative.

To discuss a real purchase, call or WhatsApp the supplied store numbers at the top of this page.

How do I place an order request?

Add a piece to your bag, choose your size and colour, then continue to checkout. The site prepares a clear WhatsApp message with your items and details.

You review that message and send it yourself. Nothing is transmitted automatically and no payment is taken online.

Browse the collection to try the flow.

Will I receive an order number?

The preview creates a local reference such as DB-261028-A1B2. It is stored in your browser only and does not confirm stock, price or delivery.

A real order reference would come from the store once availability is confirmed.

Can I order without using WhatsApp?

Yes. Call either supplied number — +254 758 102 552 or +254 707 421 337 — and request your items directly.

Why are the prices marked as illustrative?

Because the store has not supplied a confirmed price list for this preview. The amounts exist to show how the storefront behaves, not to quote you a price.

Always confirm the current price with the store before paying.

Delivery & collection

Do you deliver to Kehancha and Migori County?

That has not been confirmed for this preview. The store is based in Kehancha, Migori County, but delivery coverage, timelines and fees must be confirmed directly with the store.

How much does delivery cost?

No delivery fee is published here because none was supplied. Any fee shown at checkout is illustrative and would be replaced by the store's confirmed rates.

Can I collect my order from the shop?

The preview offers a collection option at checkout. The exact pick-up point and opening hours were not supplied, so they are not shown here — please confirm them with the store.

Payment

Can I pay on this website?

No. No payment is collected on this site and no card or M-Pesa details are requested.

Payment arrangements would be agreed directly with the store once your order is confirmed.

Is M-Pesa integrated?

Not yet. Adding M-Pesa Daraja (STK Push) so a customer can pay a confirmed order from the checkout is a recommended next step for the live build.

It requires the business to complete its own M-Pesa integration and verification, so it is not switched on in a preview.

Are there any online payment fees?

None apply today, because no online payment exists on this site. If M-Pesa or card payment is added later, the store's terms and any transaction charges would be published before you pay.

Sizes & fit

How do I know which size to choose?

The size guide lists standard body measurements in centimetres and inches for clothing and footwear.

Use it as guidance, then confirm the final size with the store — especially for fitted or oversized pieces.

What if the size is wrong?

A returns and exchange policy has not been confirmed yet. The returns page sets out proposed terms for the owner to approve or amend.

Treat that page as a starting point, not a binding policy.

About this preview

Is this the official DE LA BOSS OUTFITS website?

No. It has not been commissioned, approved, endorsed or launched by the business. It was prepared as a proposal preview so the owner can review the direction before deciding.

Read the preview notes for the full scope.

Who built this website?

When will the real shop go live?

No launch date has been scheduled. Going live depends on the owner approving the direction and supplying final products, prices, stock, photography and policies.

What is still needed to make it real?

Four things, in order:

  • Approved content — real photographs, product names, prices and stock.
  • A shared order store, so order requests reach the business instead of staying in one browser.
  • A confirmed returns, delivery and payment policy.
  • A secure dashboard login before the store dashboard is made public.

Your data

What data does this website store?

Your bag, order requests, enquiries, newsletter sign-ups and any dashboard edits are saved in your own browser using LocalStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.

The privacy page explains exactly what is stored and how to remove it.

Does the site use cookies or tracking?

No advertising or analytics trackers are installed in this preview. It uses LocalStorage, which is not a cookie and is never transmitted with web requests.

Still stuck? The fastest answer comes from the store directly. Calls and WhatsApp messages go to the supplied business numbers.
