BEFORE YOU REACH OUT.

What happens when I submit the form?

Nothing is transmitted. The note is saved in this browser under the demonstration enquiries list and a confirmation appears on screen.

Can I place a real order here?

No. The catalogue, bag and checkout are samples. To discuss a real purchase, use the supplied phone numbers or WhatsApp.

Are the store details verified?

The phone numbers and Kehancha location were supplied for this concept. Other details, including hours and social handles, are intentionally omitted.
