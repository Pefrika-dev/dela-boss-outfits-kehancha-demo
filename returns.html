HOW TO REQUEST A RETURN.

The flow is deliberately low-tech, because the store already runs on WhatsApp and phone.

  1. Check the item first. Confirm it is unused, unwashed and still has its tags and packaging.
  2. Message the store. Send the order reference, the item name and size, the reason, and a clear photograph of the item.
  3. Wait for approval. The store confirms whether the return is accepted and how it should be sent back.
  4. Return and resolve. Once the item arrives and is checked, the exchange or refund is processed.

Never send an item before approval. Unannounced returns can be lost or refused, and the store cannot be responsible for items it did not agree to receive.

FAULTY OR INCORRECT ITEMS.

If a piece arrives damaged, defective, or is not what was ordered, that is different from a change of mind. In that case the store should cover the return cost and offer a repair, replacement or refund.

Kenya's Consumer Protection Act, 2012 gives buyers rights over goods that are faulty or not as described. Those rights exist independently of whatever policy the store adopts, and no proposed term on this page is intended to override them.

Report a faulty item as soon as you notice it, with photographs. The sooner it is raised, the easier it is to resolve.
